Picky — Brand Goals
Unique Value Prop 
Picky is the group decision engine for students, built to eliminate the friction, indecision, and chaos of planning with friends.
Positioning
It helps groups choose events, food, and activities fast, fairly, and effortlessly so they can spend more time doing things together.

Brand Essence
Simple decisions. Stronger groups. More doing.


Core Values
Clarity – Choices presented simply and cleanly


Fairness – Every voice weighted equally


Momentum – Reduce friction and keep groups moving


Togetherness – Strengthen social bonds through smooth coordination


Intelligence – Smart, personalized, context-aware recommendations

Brand Identity Goals
Lightweight & Fun – Friendly, fast, and enjoyable
Effortless & Fast – Feels intuitive and one step ahead and Fast
Social at the Core – Built around friends, groups, and community
Neutral & Trusted – Unbiased referee for the group


Emotional Promise
Picky makes students feel relief, confidence, inclusion, harmony, momentum, and connection. Plans happen faster, with less stress and more excitement.

Target Audience: Students
Primary Segments
Socially Active Students – Frequently hang out, need fast decisions


Freshmen & New Students – Seeking belonging and campus discovery


Student Leaders & Organizations – Clubs, teams, fraternities, sororities


Busy, Ambitious Students – Want efficiency and time optimization


Core Needs
Make group decisions easily


Reduce indecision loops in chats


Discover high-quality events tailored to interests


Avoid missing opportunities


Strengthen group bonding


Stay efficient in a busy schedule


Key Behaviors
Plan everything in group chats
Rely on fragmented event sources: Instagram, flyers, GroupMe
Use apps recommended socially
Get frustrated by endless back-and-forth
Attend events based on proximity, vibe, and friends
Prefer spontaneous, fast decisions


Pain Points Picky Solves
“I don’t care, anything works” loops


One person always forced to choose


Missing events due to no decision


Overwhelming campus information


Too many options, no easy way to narrow


Repeating the same activities because decision fatigue wins



Brand Promise
Picky turns group planning from a chaotic problem into a smooth, fun, fair experience. It creates social momentum by helping students decide together, quickly.

The Picky Brand Should Always Feel:

Lightweight & Fun
Even complex decisions are presented in a friendly, playful way.
 The user should feel: “This is actually enjoyable.”
Social at the Core
The product revolves around people and the moments between them.
 The user should feel: “This makes my group closer.”
Effortless & Fast
The interface, flows, and brand voice must signal speed, clarity, and relief.
 The user should feel: “Thank god this took 15 seconds.”
